Breast

Breast lift (Mastopexy)

Lift and youth for your neckline

Duration
2–3 h
Recovery
2 weeks
Suggested stay
10 days

Lifts and reshapes sagging breasts, with or without implants, restoring firmness.

How the process works

  1. Virtual consultation

    Video call with the doctor, photo and medical history review. We define the ideal procedure for you.

  2. Plan & quote

    You receive your surgical plan, a detailed quote, required lab tests and available dates.

  3. Arrival in Barranquilla

    We pick you up at Ernesto Cortissoz Airport and take you to your accommodation. In-person consultation and labs.

  4. Surgery day

    Procedure in an accredited clinic with anesthesiology and nursing teams. Safety in every detail.

  5. Supported recovery

    Post-op check-ups, lymphatic drainage massages, nursing care and recovery house according to your plan.

  6. Return home & follow-up

    Medical clearance to fly and virtual follow-up from your home country until your final result.

Times are approximate. Your final plan is defined after your medical consultation.
